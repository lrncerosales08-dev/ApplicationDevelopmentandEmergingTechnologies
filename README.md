# Inception (2010)

> "Your mind is the scene of the crime."

---

## 🎬 Overview
 **Inception** is a mind-bending sci-fi heist thriller directed by **Christopher Nolan**. The story follows Dom Cobb, a professional thief who steals information by infiltrating the subconscious of his targets through their dreams.

### Key Information
 * **Director:** Christopher Nolan
 * **Release Year:** 2010
 * **Genre:** Sci-Fi / Action / Thriller
 * **Main Theme:** Regret, memory, and reality versus illusion

---

## 👥 Main Cast & Roles
 1. **Leonardo DiCaprio** as *Dom Cobb* (The Extractor)
 2. **Joseph Gordon-Levitt** as *Arthur* (The Point Man)
 3. **Elliot Page** as *Ariadne* (The Architect)
 4. **Tom Hardy** as *Eames* (The Forger)
 5. **Cillian Murphy** as *Robert Fischer* (The Target)
